Actually use Computer Use to test the game from the player perspective and try to break it.

Main rule:
Do NOT rely on developer/path context while exploring.

Main goals:
1. Debug falling off the map anywhere, especially near spawn and along map edges
2. Debug player confusion and unclear pathing, especially the park lure
3. Stop blockers / soft walls / direction aids from randomly appearing based on player position
4. Make environmental blockers feel like they were already there from the start of the level or the start of that phase
5. Keep the route authored and readable without obvious popping/spawning tricks
6. Fix anything that technically works but feels wrong from player side

Important philosophy:
- I want the neighborhood to feel intentionally built, not dynamically patched in front of the player
- Wrong paths should look naturally closed off from the start, not magically spawned shut because the player got too close
- Park lure should feel like natural curiosity + subtle funneling, not confusion
- If the player can get lost, fall out, or see the level “cheat,” that is a bug

What to test and fix:

A. Falling off map / world bounds
- Try to fall off the map from spawn
- Try to leave the playable area from every neighborhood edge
- Hug fences, roads, curbs, ramps, hedges, and corners
- Try to wedge through seams between props and blockers
- Try to stand on anything climbable and jump or slide off
- Specifically test any dark edge where the world fades into fog/blackness
- If world bounds are weak, fix them with believable environmental containment, not obvious developer walls if possible

B. Park lure confusion
- Test the park as a first-time player with no route knowledge
- Ask: why would a player go there?
- Ask: once they get there, what tells them what matters?
- Ask: what makes the park feel like the next intended step instead of just another side area?
- Fix the park lure so it feels authored and readable:
  - one strong curiosity anchor
  - one clear point of interest
  - less competing clutter
  - less ambiguity about what is important
- If the park currently feels like “there is stuff here but I don’t know why,” fix that

C. Soft blockers / direction blockers appearing randomly
- Inspect every current soft blocker system
- Find any blocker, fog wall, cloth wall, flooded-road barrier, or route gate that appears because of player position/proximity in a visibly fake way
- Remove the “random appearing” feeling
- Change blocker logic so routes feel consistently shaped and believable

Critical blocker requirement:
- I do NOT want blockers visibly appearing in front of the player because they crossed a trigger
- I want wrong routes to feel blocked from the start, or at least from the start of the current phase
- If a route changes later, the change must happen in a believable authored moment:
  - during lightning
  - during a turn-away moment
  - during a strong event beat
  - during a clearly staged world-change moment
- Never let blockers just pop into existence because the player walked near them

What I want for blocker logic:
- Static-looking route control
- Phase-aware environment shaping
- No obvious spawn-in
- No mixed messaging where a path looked open, then suddenly closes for no believable reason
- No path that looks blocked but is actually intended
- No path that looks open but is actually fake

Preferred fix direction:
- Favor persistent world shaping over reactive popping
- Favor “already blocked” wrong paths
- Favor believable environmental closure:
  - fence collapse
  - flooded road
  - wrecked car
  - police barricade
  - tarp/cloth wall
  - deep fog wall with physical support
  - overgrowth
- If something must change by phase, preload it so it feels like part of the world, then reveal/enable it in a subtle way
- Avoid visual pop-in

  - weak world bounds

  - blocker visibility toggling
  - park has no dominant point of interest
  - mixed readability
  - state machine advancing without clean staging

PHASE 3 — Fixes
- Make direct changes in scene/scripts as needed
- You may reshape the neighborhood more aggressively if necessary
- Prioritize player clarity and immersion over preserving a bad layout
- Do not add overcomplicated systems if a simpler structural fix works better

PHASE 4 — Re-test
- Re-run the exact same break attempts
- Verify:
  - cannot fall off map
  - spawn area feels contained
  - park lure is understandable
  - wrong routes feel naturally wrong
  - blockers do not visibly appear in a fake way
  - authored route still works cleanly
