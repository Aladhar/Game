Visual Bugs Found and Fixed

1. First-house facade still had extra mounted/floating clutter
- Issue:
  The first enterable house still generated a Penance relic cluster on the front-left facade.
  Even though it was thematic, it competed with the doorway during the first objective.
- Fix:
  The enterable first house no longer spawns the facade relic cluster.
  Non-enterable houses still keep relic clusters for repeated Penance material language.
- Result:
  The first house now reads cleaner as sidewalk -> porch/ramp -> doorway -> warm interior.

2. First-house floating glowing facade cards/panels
- Issue:
  The enterable house previously used floating glowing window cards in front of the facade.
  These competed with the doorway and made the front read like loose panels instead of an entrance.
- Fix:
  The enterable first house no longer spawns those window cards.
  Warm readability now comes from the doorway spill, real interior light, and table/photo light.
- Result:
  The doorway is the dominant warm read.

3. Big black slab in front of the doorway
- Issue:
  The doorway inset shadow slab read as a large black card in front of the door.
- Fix:
  Removed the doorway inset slab from the enterable-house generation.
  Kept the non-colliding doorway frame, parked-open door panel, porch, ramp, and warm interior lighting.
- Result:
  The entry looks less blocked and less cluttered.

4. Left front wall chunk visually crowded the entry
- Issue:
  The left front facade chunk was too close to the doorway and made the entrance feel narrower than it played.
- Fix:
  Shrunk/moved the first-house front wall chunks outward.
- Result:
  The opening has a cleaner read without making the house look like a missing wall.

5. Duplicate first-house wall slabs could cause z-fighting / visual overlap
- Issue:
  The first-house interior generated back/left/right wall slabs very close to the exterior shell walls.
  This can create overlapping blockout surfaces and visual flicker.
- Fix:
  Removed the duplicate interior back/left/right wall slabs.
  The exterior shell remains the wall/collision source.
- Result:
  Cleaner blockout geometry and less risk of flickering wall surfaces.

6. Rain particles could read too bright/default-white
- Issue:
  Local rain particle meshes were using default material behavior, which can read too bright in a dark horror scene.
- Fix:
  Added subtle translucent unshaded rain streak and splash materials.
- Result:
  Rain should stay present around the player without overpowering warm-house readability.

7. Debug/progression log readability
- Issue:
  BLOCKED/STATE logs were hard to read because printed pieces were concatenated tightly.
- Fix:
  Cleaned up DemoDirector logging strings.
- Result:
  Debug output is clearer when validating state flow.

8. Players could still wander into wrong map branches between objectives
- Issue:
  The hard state gates worked, but the broader neighborhood loop still allowed players to drift toward irrelevant branches and hit blockers without a strong authored reason.
- Fix:
  Added state-aware environmental soft blockers:
  - before_park blocks the park route until the first-house photo is inspected.
  - before_cul_de_sac blocks the cul-de-sac connector until the park state is complete.
  - before_church blocks the east/church branch until the cul-de-sac state is complete.
  - before_basement blocks the basement route until the church state is complete.
  - cul_de_sac_funnel temporarily blocks wrong north/south branches while guiding the player into the cul-de-sac.
  - church_funnel temporarily blocks wrong east-loop branches while guiding the player toward the church.
- Result:
  Progression now has stronger one-direction-at-a-time guidance without rebuilding the neighborhood.

9. Player could still leave/fall off the map
- Issue:
  The playable roads extended beyond parts of the general ground plane, and the map had no hard perimeter or fallback recovery.
- Fix:
  Added world-edge fog wall collision and readable chain-fence perimeter around the blockout.
  Added a fall-protection failsafe that returns the player to the last safe position or a state-appropriate fallback if they drop below the playable surface.
- Result:
  The player should no longer be able to fall endlessly off the map.

10. Cul-de-sac soft blockers could be bypassed around the road/tree trunks
- Issue:
  The cul-de-sac funnel blockers were too road-focused, so a player could route around them through lawn/edge space.
- Fix:
  Widened the cul-de-sac funnel blockers and added north/south lawn no-bypass blockers around the cul-de-sac approach.
- Result:
  The park-to-cul-de-sac stage now funnels the player more reliably toward the intended cul-de-sac route.

11. Houses read too small for the suburban horror blockout
- Issue:
  The 10x8m house masses made the neighborhood feel small and less oppressive.
- Fix:
  Increased non-enterable houses to larger 12x10m blockout masses, enlarged roofs, and expanded the first enterable house shell, porch, floor, and ceiling while keeping its doorway and objective path clear.
- Result:
  Houses now feel more substantial without breaking the first-house route.

Potential Visual Risks To Watch In Manual Play

1. Church still reads like a solid blockout mass
- Current state:
  The church/community center is functionally usable for the demo route, but it is still a simple blockout mass.
- Risk:
  A player may expect the front doors to open like the first house.
- Recommendation:
  Later, carve or fake a clearer side/basement entry read. Not game-breaking for the current route.

2. Route gates are intentionally blunt
- Current state:
  Route blockers are visible wet-cloth walls until each state unlocks.
- Risk:
  They are clear for demo control but may feel artificial.
- Recommendation:
  Later, replace with more authored blockers like fallen fences, floodwater, police tape, locked gates, or darkness walls.

3. First-house manual subjective readability still needs viewport review
- Current state:
  Automated checks confirm the clutter nodes are gone and collision/path checks pass.
- Risk:
  Final judgment of mood, composition, and silhouette strength still needs real camera screenshots/playtest.
- Recommendation:
  Do one live viewport pass when Godot can be run again.
