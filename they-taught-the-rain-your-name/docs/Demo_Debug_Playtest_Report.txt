Pass 1: Start/spawn and first destination read

Issue:
First lit house facade looked like detached construction panels instead of one believable wall.

What the player does:
Approaches the first warm house from the road and looks at the entrance.

What breaks / feels wrong:
The front left/right wall chunks, doorframe pieces, and stacked trim above the doorway read as separate floating blockout slabs.

Why it is bad:
The first house is the first authored destination. If the entrance reads messy, players may hesitate or think the doorway is blocked.

How severe it is:
MAJOR

Suggested fix:
Rebuild the front as a simple structural facade: one lower wall split around the doorway, one clear rectangular doorway, one lintel/header, one second-story face, built-in jambs, and one roof overhang.
