# Story Rough Draft

## Title

THEY TAUGHT THE RAIN YOUR NAME

## Premise

The player is an emergency radio technician sent to repair a rural storm warning tower outside Greyhollow County after reports that the broadcast is repeating voices of people who died years ago.

The warning system is not broken.

It is warning people in the voices of the dead.

## Opening

The player drives through heavy rain.

The emergency broadcast says:

> This is a severe weather warning for Greyhollow County.  
> Do not answer repeated sounds.  
> Do not follow voices in rainfall.  
> If you hear your name, remain indoors.

The radio glitches.

A voice says the player’s name.

The road sign reads:

```text
GREYHOLLOW
Population: 1,106
```

Lightning flashes.

The sign now reads:

```text
Population: 1,107
```

## Central Truth

The rain learned to imitate people.

At first, Greyhollow created rituals to survive:
- never answer your name in the rain
- never open after three knocks
- never follow footsteps that match yours
- never pray out loud during a storm
- never ring bells unless you want to be heard

Over time, survival rituals became preservation rituals.

The town taught names and patterns to the rain so the dead would not disappear.

But the rain does not preserve souls.

It preserves repeated behavior.

## Theme

If something can copy your voice, your footsteps, your habits, and your reactions, how much of you does it need before it becomes you?

## Main Enemies

### Penance Carrier

The ritual archivist. Carries a shrine made from sound-making objects: bells, keys, radios, clocks, toys, prayer beads, chains, door knockers, wind chimes, and broken emergency equipment.

It gathers repeated behaviors and gives them back to the rain.

### Bell Saint

The warning system that became a threat. It rings patterns that once protected the town. Later, the rain imitates the bells.

### Rain Hunter

A tracker who follows movement noise: sprinting, mud steps, breathing, gates, and puddles.

### Lantern Bride

A preserved pattern of distress and longing. She lures through crying, humming, and soft speech.

### Club Man

The rhythm of enforcement from the old mill. He reacts to repeated timing and panic rhythm.

### Crooked Scarecrow

The guardian of silence. Carries the Quiet Blade. Moves when silence becomes unnatural.

## Endings

### Silence Ending
You shut down the tower. The dead patterns vanish.

### Archive Ending
You preserve the storm archive. The town survives, but the storm spreads.

### Release Ending
You release the stored sounds. The rain becomes normal, but the voices are gone.

### Name Ending
You answer your name. Your pattern joins the rain.

### Defiance Ending
You broadcast your own name to draw the storm away from Greyhollow.
