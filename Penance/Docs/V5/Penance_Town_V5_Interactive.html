A real place before the horror

The mill crossing predates the civic street. Orchard cottages grew west of it; postwar homes climbed Briar Rise. Church land and the Foster estate share the older ridge road.

Six months after the Quieting, the town is intact. Interrupted routines and a few working lights make the absence conspicuous.

Readable geography

The curved creek separates everyday town life from the church and estate. One public bridge is the dependable crossing. A closed older footbridge reserves a later shortcut.
