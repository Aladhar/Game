For Penance himself as the enemy character, I’d use a slightly more advanced humanoid armature than the MC, because enemies usually need more exaggerated animation: lunges, grabs, crawling/jerky movement, attacks, head tracking, and hit reactions.
Best choice:
Use a Rigify Human Meta-Rig, but modify it for enemy movement
Start with:
Blender → Add → Armature → Human Meta-Rig
Then build it as a game-ready humanoid rig with:
Root / global control
Pelvis / hips
Spine controls
Chest control
Neck + head
Jaw bone, if his face opens/screams
Clavicles
IK arms
IK legs
Pole targets for elbows/knees
Finger bones
Twist bones for upper arms and forearms
Extra shoulder/back helper bones if he has unnatural posture
For Penance, I would not use a clean “normal human only” rig. I’d make him humanoid, but with extra control for horror-style movement.
Best bind pose for Penance
Use a wide A-pose, not a perfect T-pose.
Recommended pose:
Arms angled down around 35–45°
Elbows slightly bent
Hands away from body
Fingers slightly curled/separated
Spine mostly straight, but rig can support hunching
Feet shoulder-width apart
Knees slightly relaxed
This prevents shoulder stretching and keeps the hands away from the torso/legs during animations.
Extra bones I would add for Penance
Since he is the enemy, add these if you want better control:
1. Spine/hunch controls
Use more spine bones than the MC.
Instead of only:
pelvis → spine → chest
Use:
pelvis → lower spine → mid spine → chest → upper chest → neck → head
This lets him hunch, twist, lean, and twitch without the whole torso bending like one block.
2. Shoulder/clavicle controls
Very important for creepy enemy attacks.
Penance probably needs:
raised shoulders
hunched posture
reaching attacks
arm dragging
sudden grabs
So make sure the clavicle bones are placed well and weighted cleanly.
3. Finger bones
For the MC, fingers are nice.
For Penance, fingers are required if he grabs, points, claws, or reaches.
Use:
3 bones per finger
3 bones for thumb if possible
Optional fingertip control bones
Do not leave enemy hands as stiff mitten hands.
4. Jaw/head control
If Penance has creepy facial movement, add at least:
head bone
jaw bone
eye target bones if needed
Even if you do not fully facial-rig him, a jaw bone helps a lot for horror enemy animations.
5. Twist bones
Use twist bones especially for:
upper arms
forearms
thighs
calves
This prevents the arms and legs from collapsing when he twists during attacks.
Best overall setup
For Penance, I’d do:
Rigify Human Meta-Rig + IK arms/legs + finger controls + extra spine bones + twist bones + jaw/head control.
That gives the most control without destroying the mesh.
For animation style
Use this rig style if Penance moves like:
slow stalking enemy
sudden lunging enemy
twitchy/possessed enemy
grabbing enemy
crawling or bent-over enemy
The extra spine and shoulder controls matter more than anything else.
What to avoid
Do not use a super basic skeleton with only:
one spine bone
simple arms/legs
no IK
no fingers
no twist bones
That will animate, but it will look stiff and the shoulders/arms will distort badly during attacks.
Final recommendation
For Penance himself:
Use a modified Rigify humanoid rig in a wide relaxed A-pose, with IK limbs, full finger bones, twist bones, extra spine controls, clavicle controls, and optional jaw/head/eye controls.
